GateFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GateFit ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

GateFit is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from users of the App.

Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use third-party analytics services to monitor your behavior within the App.
  • We do not display advertisements or share data with advertising networks.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Any information you enter into GateFit — such as your selected exercises, completed sessions, or in-app preferences — is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party. If you delete the App, this data is deleted along with it.

3. Third-Party Services

GateFit does not integrate with third-party services that collect user data. The App does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or similar tracking services.

4. Children's Privacy

GateFit is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, this applies to users of all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

6. Your Rights

Because GateFit does not collect any personal data, there is no personal data for you to access, correct, delete, or export. You can delete all App-related data at any time by deleting the App from your device.
